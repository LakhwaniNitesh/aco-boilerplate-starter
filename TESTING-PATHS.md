## 💡 DECISION CRITERIA

**Pick FAST TRACK if:**
- ⏰ You have < 10 minutes
- 🎯 You just want to verify code works
- 🚀 You're in a hurry
- 🔍 You don't need detailed understanding

**Pick STRUCTURED TRACK if:**
- ⏰ You have 30-40 minutes
- 🎯 You want to understand the flow
- 🧪 You want systematic validation
- 📚 You want to learn as you test

**Pick TECHNICAL TRACK if:**
- ⏰ You have 1-2 hours
- 🎯 You're a developer/architect
- 🏗️ You need deep understanding
- 🔧 You'll be modifying/extending code

**Pick EXPERT TRACK if:**
- ⏰ You have 2+ hours
- 🎯 You're leading the team
- 📖 You need complete knowledge
- 👥 You'll be training others
